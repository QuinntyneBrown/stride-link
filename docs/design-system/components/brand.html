Brand lockup

The belt-and-arcs mark with the Stride Link wordmark: one accessible image, orange arcs in both themes.

Overview

The brand lockup is the belt-and-arcs mark followed by the wordmark Stride (Inter Tight 800) Link (Inter Tight 400), taken from the Stride Link logo sheet. It appears once per page, in the top bar. It is an image of the product name, not a link and not a heading.

  • The mark is a belt (rounded rectangle) with two rollers; the drive roller and the two broadcast arcs are signal orange.
  • The outline and idler roller use currentColor (--color-brand-ink), so the mark follows the text colour into dark mode.
  • The arcs and drive roller stay --color-brand-signal (signal-500) in both themes, and become CanvasText in forced-colours mode.

Anatomy

  1. Belt outline and idler roller.mark__ink stroke and .mark__ink-fill, currentColor.
  2. Drive roller and arcs.mark__signal-fill and .mark__signal, --color-brand-signal.
  3. WordmarkStride 800 + Link 400, letter-spacing --letter-spacing-wordmark (-0.035em), 0.18em between words.

Responsive behaviour

The lockup never shrinks or hides the wordmark; at 320 px it takes about 130 px of the top bar, leaving room for the short pill (Offline). The SVG keeps overflow: visible so the arcs are never clipped.

Theming

Two tokens: --color-brand-ink (currentColor) and --color-brand-signal (signal-500). The wordmark uses --color-fg-default through .brand. Dark mode mirrors the logo sheet's reversed lockup: paper on ink, orange unchanged.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Shipped: <span class="brand" role="img" aria-label="Stride Link"> with the SVG and wordmark both aria-hidden, so it is announced once as an image named Stride Link.

Content

  • The product name is always Stride Link: two words, both capitalised. Never StrideLink, Stride-Link or BeltCheck (the repository name).
  • Do not add a tagline to the lockup.
