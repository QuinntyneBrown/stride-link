Panel

The titled section card that holds each region of the page, in the L2-064 reading order.

Overview

A panel is a titled <section> card. Every region of the page is one: status (with the verdict), connection, telemetry, belt controls and the packet log. Panels give the page its reading order (L2-064) and its landmarks: each is labelled by its own title through aria-labelledby.

  • The title is a small uppercase overline (.panel__title), visually quiet so the content leads.
  • Actions sit at the end in .panel__actions.
  • Panels never nest, and they never scroll themselves except the log panel's inner scroller.

The shared kit's .card (in the mocks' ui.css, with a shadow) is used by neither set; the panel replaces it.

Anatomy

  1. Header.panel__header: title on the left, optional meta (count, badge) on the right; wraps.
  2. Title<h2 class="panel__title">, overline style, muted.
  3. BodyAny components, 16 px apart (gap: var(--space-4)).
  4. Actions.panel__actions: buttons share the row below 576 px.

Variants

Variants are about the header and the job, not the look; every panel has the same surface.

    Responsive behaviour

    • Below 576 px buttons in .panel__actions stretch to share the row; from 576 px they keep their width.
    • Padding grows from 20 to 24 px at 768 px.
    • From 992 px the log panel becomes sticky and fills the viewport height under the top bar (minus the Stop bar while the belt is active); its scroller then grows to fill it (L2-066).
    • Long content (device names, hex) wraps inside; panels set min-width: 0 so grid columns never overflow.

    Theming

    Component tokens --panel-bg (--color-bg-surface), --panel-border (--color-border-default) and --panel-padding. Panels carry no shadow; in dark the surface (ink-850) sits one step above the canvas (ink-900).

    Accessibility

    Role and pattern

    <section aria-labelledby> gives each panel a region landmark named by its title. The page's <h1> is inside the status panel (shipped) or above the panels (sets A and B); panel titles are <h2>.

    Keyboard

    KeyAction
    NoneThe panel itself is not focusable; its controls are reached in DOM order.

    Focus

    Focus order follows panel order: status, connection, telemetry, belt, log (L2-060, L2-064).

    Labelling

    Each panel's title id is referenced by its aria-labelledby; titles are unique on the page.

    Contrast

    Content

    • Shipped titles: Connection, Telemetry, Belt controls, Packet log. The status panel's heading is the page <h1>, Treadmill smoke test.
    • The mocks title the first panel Status; set A's page heading is Connectivity smoke test, set B's and the shipped one is Treadmill smoke test.
    • Titles are nouns, sentence case in the source (the overline style uppercases them visually).
