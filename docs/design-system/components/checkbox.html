Checkbox

One checkbox: the belt-gate interlock, I am standing clear of the belt. A safety control with a 44 px box.

Overview

The checkbox (.choice) is a native checkbox restyled with tokens and wrapped in its label. Stride Link has exactly one: the belt-gate interlock, I am standing clear of the belt (L2-042). It is a safety interlock, so it is documented here with its constraints, not as a general-purpose control.

  • Offered only under a Talks or Unlocked verdict; absent under Silent, Locked or no verdict (L2-042 criteria 1 and 4).
  • Unticked at the start of every run (criterion 5).
  • Ticking it reveals Start, speed and incline; unticking hides them again, and Stop stays visible while the belt is active (criterion 6).
  • It never sends a frame itself.

Never weaken it: no “remember my choice”, no pre-ticked state, no auto-tick (AGENTS.md belt interlocks).

Anatomy

  1. Gate box.belt-gate: raised surface, 1px --color-border-strong, --radius-md, 12 × 16 px padding.
  2. CheckboxShipped: 44 × 44 px (--target-min), --radius-xs; mocks: 20 px inside a 44 px row.
  3. LabelSemibold body text; clicking it toggles the box.

Variants

Two variants of the same control.

Belt gate (shipped): the box itself is a 44 × 44 px target.

L2-061 measures the bounding box of each interactive element, and a 20 px input fails that measurement even inside a 44 px label. That is why the shipped gate enlarges the input itself.

Both themes

  • Hover changes only the cursor; there is no hover colour (neither the mocks nor the shipped kit draw one), so the matrix has no hover column.
  • Disabled is shown for completeness: the product never disables the gate. It hides it instead.
  • Indeterminate and invalid do not apply: a safety interlock is either ticked or not.

Responsive behaviour

  • The gate box spans its column at every width; the label wraps beside the 44 px box at 320 px.
  • It sits after the safety list and before the belt controls in the one-column order (L2-064).

Theming

Unchecked: --color-bg-surface fill with a --color-border-strong edge. Checked: --color-accent fill with a --color-fg-on-accent tick. The gate box is --color-bg-surface-raised. In dark the raised surface lightens (ink-800), the accent stays signal-500 and the tick stays ink.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native <input type="checkbox"> inside its <label>, following the WAI-ARIA Authoring Practices Checkbox pattern (two-state) with no ARIA.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves to the checkbox.
SpaceToggles it. Ticking reveals Start, speed and incline after it in the Tab order.
EscWhile the belt is active, sends Stop (L2-045), whatever the checkbox state.

Focus

Shared ring around the box. When the controls are hidden by unticking, focus stays on the checkbox, so it is never lost into removed content.

Labelling

The label text is the accessible name and is fixed by L2-042: I am standing clear of the belt. The shipped page fills it from GATE_LABEL.

The checked fill clears 3:1 by a small margin in light (3.3:1 on the raised box, 3.15:1 on the panel); it carries the tick in ink, which is the primary signal.

Motion

Fill and border transition 120 ms; none under reduced motion.

Touch

The shipped box is 44 × 44 px (L2-061) and the whole label is also tappable.

Content

  • The label is exactly I am standing clear of the belt: first person, a statement of fact, no trailing full stop (L2-042).
  • Never reword it into a question or an instruction; acceptance tests match it character for character.
