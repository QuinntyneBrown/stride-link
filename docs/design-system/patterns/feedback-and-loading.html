Pattern

Feedback and loading

One primary channel per event: the status line says where the run is, the verdict says how it ended, alerts interrupt only for safety.

Overview

Every event on this page has exactly one primary channel. The tester is usually standing beside a treadmill, glancing at a phone or laptop, so feedback must be readable at a glance, in words, and never only in colour (L2-062). There are no optimistic updates and no undo: the treadmill is the source of truth, and the page reports what the treadmill did.

Status lineWhere the run is: checking, ready, connecting, init, waiting, connected, disconnected, and the L2 error sentencesrole="status", politeYes

Spinner versus progress: the only indeterminate wait the tester can see is the browser chooser scanning, drawn with a spinner for flow context. Waits the page owns (the 20-second Silent window, the 2.5-second acknowledgement window) are known in length; set A shows them as a progress bar, set B and the shipped page show only the status sentence. Skeletons are not used: nothing loads from a network.

Responsive behaviour

  • The status line and verdict are first in reading order at every width (L2-064), so feedback is the first thing on a phone.
  • At 992 px and up the log moves to the right column; the status line stays at the top of the left column.
  • Set A's progress meta row keeps both labels on one line with justify-content: space-between and wraps below 360 px.
  • Toasts (set A) sit bottom-right from 640 px and full width below, above the Stop bar when it is shown.

Accessibility

  • Status and verdict changes go through aria-live="polite" (L2-036, L2-063); telemetry is deliberately not live.
  • Only two things are assertive: the connection-lost alert and Stop failed. Both are safety messages that must interrupt.
  • The log is role="log" with aria-live="off": hundreds of frames a minute would drown every other announcement.
  • The busy dot pulses (.dot--pulse); under reduced motion it stops. The words carry the state, so nothing is lost (L2-062, L2-063).
  • Set A's progress bar has role="progressbar" with aria-valuenow in seconds and the same text visible beside it.

Content

  • Status sentences are full sentences with a full stop, or an ellipsis while something is under way: Connecting to the treadmill….
  • Failure sentences are the L2 wording, character for character, and say what to do next: Couldn't connect within 10 seconds. Move closer to the treadmill and try again.
  • Never announce success twice: a toast that repeats the verdict (set A's verdict toast) adds noise to the polite region.
  • Times are given with units and no false precision: 20 s, 2.5 s, 12.4 s of 20 s.
