Foundation

Shape

Five radii that grow with the element, a pill, and three border widths.

  • Overview
  • Radii
  • Border widths
  • Usage
  • Accessibility
  • Tokens

Overview

Rounder than the starter kit, after the 16 px cards and the rounded icon tile on the logo sheet. Radius grows with the element: controls md, panels lg, sheets xl. Five radii plus full; three border widths.

Radii

--radius-xs4px
--radius-sm6px
--radius-md10px
--radius-lg16px
--radius-xl20px
--radius-fullpill

Border widths

Hairline 1 px (every edge) · thick 2 px (focus ring width, key-cap bottom, spinner) · accent 4 px (verdict and alert left bar).

Usage

  • Nest radii: a child inside a padded parent uses the next size down (metric md inside panel lg).
  • The 4 px accent bar marks tone on the left of the verdict card and alerts; it is always paired with a word.
  • The starter kit's one off-scale radius (2px 2px 0 0 on tabs) is not part of this system: tabs are not used.

Accessibility

  • Shape never carries meaning on its own. The accent bar, dots and pills always sit beside text.
  • Borders that identify a control use --color-border-strong at 3:1; hairline panel borders are decorative.

Tokens

TokenValueUse
--radius-xsCheckbox
--radius-smFocus ring corners, code, key caps, menu items
--radius-mdButtons, inputs, alerts, toasts, verdict, metrics, log
--radius-lgPanels, dialogs from 640 px
--radius-xlDialog sheet top corners below 640 px
--radius-fullPrivacy pill, badges, dots, verdict icon disc
--border-width-hairlineEvery edge
--border-width-thickKey-cap bottom, spinner
--border-width-accentVerdict and alert accent bar
