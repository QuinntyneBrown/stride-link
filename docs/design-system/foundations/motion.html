Foundation

Motion

Short, functional transitions, two activity loops, and nothing at all under reduced motion.

  • Overview
  • Durations
  • Easings
  • Loops
  • Choreography
  • Accessibility and reduced motion
  • Usage

Overview

Motion is small and functional: state changes ease in over 120–320 ms, two loops show activity (the pulsing busy dot and the scanning spinner). Nothing moves for decoration. Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce the duration tokens drop to 0 ms and the shipped stylesheet removes every animation and transition (L2-063).

Durations

Press a button to replay. With reduced motion on, the dot jumps.

Easings

Loops

Connecting to the treadmill…Scanning for devices…
Pulse (1.6 s) on the busy dot; spin (0.8 s linear) on the spinner. Both stop under reduced motion; the words remain.
HTML
<span class="status status--busy"><span class="dot dot--pulse" aria-hidden="true"></span>Connecting to the treadmill…</span><span class="inline-status"><span class="spinner" aria-hidden="true"></span>Scanning for devices…</span>

Choreography

  • Enter with --ease-enter, leave with --ease-exit, change in place with --ease-standard.
  • Dialogs scale in from 96 % on wide screens and slide up as a sheet on phones; toasts slide up 16 px; the backdrop fades.
  • Telemetry and log lines update without animation: they change many times a second and must stay readable.
  • Nothing animates the Stop bar: it must be usable the instant it appears.

Accessibility and reduced motion

  • tokens.css sets --duration-fast/base/slow/deliberate to 0 ms under reduced motion.
  • Shipped: components.css also sets animation: none !important; transition: none !important under reduced motion, so loops stop as well (L2-063: no animation or transition longer than 0 ms). The mocks only shortened animations to 0 s.
  • No flashing; the pulse fades opacity between 1 and 0.35 well under three times a second (WCAG 2.3.1).
  • Smooth scrolling is turned off under reduced motion.

Usage

  • Use a duration token; never a raw time except the two loop periods.
  • Never convey a state by motion alone: the busy dot sits next to a sentence.
  • Keep transitions on colour, shadow and transform; never animate layout.

Tokens

TokenValueUse
--duration-instantNo transition
--duration-fastButton, input, link and skip-link transitions
--duration-baseBackdrop fade, toast enter and leave
--duration-slowDialog enter, progress width
--duration-deliberateReserved
