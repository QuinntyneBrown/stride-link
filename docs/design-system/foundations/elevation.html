Foundation

Elevation

Mostly flat: four shadows for what floats, a surface hierarchy, and a z-order with a Stop bar layer.

Overview

Stride Link is mostly flat: panels sit on the canvas with a hairline edge. Shadows mark the few things that float: the primary button on hover, the Stop bar, toasts and the skip link, dialogs. Four levels plus the focus shadow. In dark the shadow colour turns pure black at higher opacity, and raised surfaces get lighter instead of relying on shadow.

--z-tooltip1600Skip link

The Stop bar has its own layer (900) so it stays over every page part while the belt is active (L2-045) yet under the browser-drawn chooser and any dialog. A component never sets a raw z-index.

Usage

  • Do not add shadow to panels; the hairline border is the separation.
  • A floating element takes one level; never stack two shadows.
  • Use the z tokens; never a raw number.

Accessibility

  • Elevation is never the only cue for an overlay: dialogs also have a backdrop, a title and focus moved into them.
  • Fixed layers (Stop bar, toasts) never cover the focused element: the body reserves the Stop bar's height (WCAG 2.4.11, L2-064).
