Foundation

Iconography

Inline 24-unit stroke icons in currentColor, always next to words; the shipped page uses two.

  • Overview
  • Icon sheet
  • Duplicate drawings
  • Sizes
  • The brand mark

Overview

Icons are inline SVG on a 24-unit grid, 2-unit round-capped strokes, no fill, drawn in currentColor so they take the text or tone colour. They are never the only carrier of meaning: every icon sits next to words. The shipped page uses exactly two drawings: the Stop belt square (filled) and the brand mark.

Sizes

.icon--sm 16 px (privacy pill), .icon 20 px (default, alerts, toasts), .icon--lg 24 px (verdict, Stop belt). In buttons icons are 1.25em.

The brand mark

StrideLink
A belt (rounded rectangle) with two rollers and two broadcast arcs. The outline and left roller follow the text colour; the right roller and arcs are signal orange.

The mark is drawn on a 72 × 56 grid, not the icon grid, and is never used as an icon. See Brand lockup.

Usage

  • Markup: <svg class="icon" aria-hidden="true" focusable="false" viewBox="0 0 24 24">…</svg>.
  • One meaning per icon across the page (WCAG 3.2.4). The download arrow always means Export log.
  • Tone icons take the tone's --color-*-icon token; on toasts the -icon-on-inverse token.
  • Do not add icon fonts or external sprites: the page is one self-contained file.

Accessibility

  • Decorative icons are aria-hidden="true" and focusable="false"; the adjacent text is the name.
  • An icon-only control (toast dismiss) carries aria-label.
  • Icons that carry tone are at least 3:1 against their background (checked pairs: alert icons, verdict icons, toast icons on inverse).
  • In forced-colours mode icons follow currentColor, so they turn to system colours with the text.
